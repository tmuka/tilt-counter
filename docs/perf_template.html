Tilt Counter Wiring

A compact layout for the red/blue tilt counter, using a 10 × 10 hole corner of the 10 × 24 hole (3×7 cm) board from your perfboard kit. Every pad on these boards is separate, so all connections are solder bridges and bare wire on the back.

Board layout

Build it in this order

Mark row 1 and column A on the board with a marker before you start. That makes it easy to find holes again after you flip the board over.

  1. Chip socket

    Pins in D4–D7 and G4–G7, with the notch toward row 10, away from the LED edge. Pin 1 is then at G7. Solder two opposite corners, check it sits flat, then solder the rest. Keep the chip out until the last step.

  2. Resistors, standing up

    Stand each resistor on end and fold its top lead down beside the body. The body goes on the first hole listed.

    • 1 kΩ (brown-black-red, red LED): body on C4, lead in C3.
    • 470 Ω (yellow-violet-brown, blue LED): body on H4, lead in H3. This mirrors the 1 kΩ.
    • 10 kΩ (brown-black-orange): body on G8, lead in G10, skipping G9.

  3. LEDs and capacitor

    • Red LED: long leg (+) in C2, short leg (flat side) in B2.
    • Blue LED: long leg in H2, short leg in I2.
    • 0.1 µF ("104"): H9 and J9, either way round.

    Long legs face inward and flat sides face the board edges. Push both LEDs down to the same height.

  4. Buses on the back

    • − bus: one U-shaped bare wire. Start at A6, go up column A to A1, along row 1 to J1, then down column J to J10. Along row 1, solder it only at A1, B1, F1, I1 and J1; leave the other row 1 pads unsoldered. Down columns A and J, solder at every pad.
    • + bus: row 10, from D10 to I10.

  5. Solder bridges

    • Red LED: D4–C4, C3–C2, and B2–B1
    • Tilt A (chip pin 7): D6–C6–B6
    • Power (chip pin 8): D7–D8–D9–D10
    • Ground (chip pin 4): G4–F4–F3–F2–F1. This runs up column F to the − bus; F4 is under the socket, but its pad is reachable from the back.
    • Blue LED (chip pin 3): G5–H5–H4, then H3–H2 and I2–I1
    • Tilt B (chip pin 2): G6–H6–I6
    • Reset (chip pin 1): G7–H7–I7, and G7–G8
    • Capacitor to +: H9–H10

  6. Off-board wires

    • Tilt switch A (red score): B6 and A6, on the left edge.
    • Tilt switch B (blue score): I6 and J6, on the right edge.
    • Reset button: I7 and J7, using diagonally opposite legs.
    • Battery holder: red to I10, black to J10. Leave its switch off.

  7. Check before fitting the chip

    • Hold the board up to a light and look over the back for accidental bridges, starting with I10/J10.
    • With a meter on continuity: I10 must not beep to J10. Socket pin 8 (D7) should beep to I10, and pin 4 (G4) to J10.
    • Batteries in, switch on: about 4.5 V between D7 and G4. Switch off again.

  8. Fit the chip and test

    The chip needs the firmware with the updated pin assignment (tilt A on pin 7, blue LED on pin 3, tilt B on pin 2). Press it into the socket with its notch (or dot) toward row 10, so pin 1 sits at G7. Switch on. Both LEDs give a tiny blip, which is the zero count. Tilt switch A and the red LED lights solid, then blinks out the count.
